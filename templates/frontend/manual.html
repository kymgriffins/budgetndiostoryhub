Getting Started

What is Budget Ndio Story?

Budget Ndio Story is a Kenyan budget transparency platform designed to help citizens understand and track government spending. We turn national and county budgets into clear insights, practical analysis, and trackable evidence.

The platform provides educational content, budget tracking tools, and community engagement features to promote financial transparency and civic education.

Fun Fact! 💡

Budget Ndio means "The Budget Story" in Swahili - reflecting our mission to make government finances understandable for everyone.

Platform Features
  • Video Content Library Educational videos explaining budget concepts in simple terms
  • Blog & Articles In-depth articles on financial literacy and budget analysis
  • Budget Tracking Track government spending at national and county levels
  • Community Forum Discuss budget topics with other engaged citizens
  • Newsletter Stay updated with weekly budget insights
How to Navigate the Platform

The platform uses a modern sidebar navigation system. Here's how to get around:

  1. Desktop: Use the left sidebar to navigate between sections
  2. Mobile: Tap the hamburger menu (☰) at the top left to open the sidebar
  3. Search: Use the search bar at the top to find content quickly
  4. Quick Actions: Look for the + buttons for quick access to create new content

Authentication Guide

Creating an Account

Follow these steps to create your account:

  1. Click Register on the login page or navigate to /register/
  2. Enter your First Name and Last Name
  3. Choose a unique Username (this will be displayed publicly)
  4. Enter a valid Email Address
  5. Create a Password (must be 8+ characters with uppercase, lowercase, and number)
  6. Confirm your password
  7. Accept the Terms of Service
  8. Click Create Account
💡 Pro Tip:

Use a password manager to generate and store strong passwords. Avoid using the same password across multiple sites.

Resetting Your Password

Forgot your password? No worries! Here's how to reset it:

  1. Click Forgot password? on the login page
  2. Enter the email address you used to register
  3. Click Reset Password
  4. Check your email for a reset link
  5. Click the link to open the password reset page
  6. Enter and confirm your new password
  7. Click Reset Password to save

Check Your Spam! 📧

If you don't see the reset email within 5 minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
